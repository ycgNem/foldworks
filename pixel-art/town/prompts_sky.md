**Generations used: 0.** Every town sprite of this pass is drawn by hand in the build scripts
here (`build.py`): the six houses were redrawn from scratch for daylight (the revamp's PixelLab
houses, stall and lamp, `pixel-art/revamp/raw/b32` frames 8-15, are no longer used by any town
sprite), the promenade, its railing and the grass tufts are new, and Unit 7's observatory and
Dame Voltaire's pavilion are their Milestone 4 drawings set on stone and marble footings.
