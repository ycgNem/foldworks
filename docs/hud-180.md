# The 180 (2026-10-21): text and HUD turned away from Last Watt

The owner, 2026-10-21: *"take a look at the game Last Watt, all the text in this game mirrors it,
I want to do a 180 to that game's format, revamp completely."* Asked how far, they picked **text
and the whole HUD**: a new look and voice for every word, and a screen with no numbered bottom
hotbar and no top strip of counters, building from a wheel at the cursor and numbers living on the
floor. Then: decide the rest, run autonomously, commit. This doc is the plan every change follows;
`DECISIONS.md` ("The 180") records it. It replaces `docs/ui-redesign.md`'s screen (that doc's
lessons stand: one loud thing at a time, the floor is the star, keep the juice).

Last Watt is the owner's tower defense (`C:\Users\jacks\Documents\candlepower`). Both games grew
from the Sprocket & Sprout engine layer, so they read as one game. Compare against its screenshots
(`candlepower/e2e/out/midrun-bot.png`, `help.png`, `critic5/01-tut-step0.png`).

## What mirrors Last Watt now (all of it goes)

| # | Last Watt and Foldworks today | The 180 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The S&S proportional bitmap font, 1 px strokes, cap 7, and its 5 px small caps | Three new Foldworks fonts, **bold** (2 px vertical strokes), drawn for this game |
| 2 | Light text on dark slate rectangles, 1 px border, a gold UPPERCASE title | **Dark ink on warm paper**: tags on strings, speech bubbles, ribbons, a ledger book |
| 3 | Mechanics explained in sentences with numbers inside them, colon chains, semicolons | **Show, don't tell**: icon equations, bars, chips; sentences only in a character's mouth |
| 4 | Key hints in brackets: "(T)", "[ENTER]", "Click it to…" | **Keycaps** drawn as little keys: `{k:T}`, `{k:LMB}` |
| 5 | A top strip of counters with small-caps labels (LIGHTS 20 / MONEY 120 / kW …) | **No strip**: the Purse (coin only), everything else on the floor |
| 6 | A bottom row of numbered slots with prices under them, in grouped frames | **No hotbar**: the Workbench Wheel opens at the cursor |
| 7 | Cards of instructions in a column (FIRST STEPS / THE LAST LIGHT 1/18) | **The cast talks**: Bramble's speech bubble is the goal; others speak their own lines |
| 8 | A help screen: a two-column table of keys | Keys live on keycaps where you need them; the notebook's "Keys" page is a drawn keyboard |
| 9 | Square 2x2 patches of round lumps | Outcrops with their own geology (done: "The ore revamp") |

## The rules every piece passes

1. **Ink on paper.** HUD text is dark ink (`T.ink`) on paper (`T.paper`, cream). Light text
   only where it floats over the floor (coin pops, tags' numbers drawn with an ink outline) and in
   celebrations. Contrast on paper: `ink` and `inkSoft` at least 7:1, every accent ink at least
   4.4:1 (a test checks the pairs).
2. **No explaining sentence outside a mouth.** Reports, tags, the wheel, menus and cards show
   mechanics as rows: `{i:ironOre} → {i:ironIngot}  30/min`, a bar with a notch at the target, a
   chip (`{bad:CHOKED}`), a keycap verb (`{k:LMB} shovel`). A line of prose is allowed only inside a
   speech bubble (a character talking) or as flavour text (italic-free, one line, the item's
   personality), and stays under ~14 words. No semicolons anywhere a player reads. At most one
   colon per line. No parentheses around keys or prices.
3. **Nothing is lost.** Pillar 3 holds: every fact a hover report gives today (what it waits for,
   who feeds it and how much, where its byproduct goes, what it lacks and by how much, power, mode,
   Overdrive) is still there, drawn as rows. A shorter report that hides a bottleneck is a bug.
4. **The cast talks.** Goals, hints, lessons and offers are speech bubbles from a portrait, in that
   character's voice (the voice guide below). The default speaker is Mayor Bramble.
5. **The floor carries the numbers.** A rate or progress number sits on the thing it measures, as
   a tag on the floor: the Dock, the Hopper, a line's delivery, a grid, the Bay, an audit box.
6. **Build at the cursor.** The Workbench Wheel, opened where the mouse is.
7. **Keep the juice** (`docs/ui-redesign.md`, memory "full but organized"): rolling and bumping
   numbers, flying coins, bursts, ribbons, the stamp, confetti; one loud thing at a time.

## Typography (`src/ui/font.ts`)

Three fonts, all new glyph tables, all bold, none sharing a glyph shape with the S&S font:

| Font | Use | Cap | x-height | Strokes | Digits | Line |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Reed** (body; `drawText`, `textWidth`, `wrapText`, `FONT_H`, `LINE_H`) | everything you read | 7 | 5 | 2 px verticals, 1 px horizontals, rounded bowls (no square corners), open apertures, single-storey `a` and `g`, a hooked `t`, a round dot on `i`/`j` | tabular, 5 wide | 11 |
| **Bullfrog** (display; new `drawBig`, `bigWidth`, `BIG_H`) | the Purse, titles, names, grade letters, big numbers, button words | 9 | 7 | 2 px everywhere, soft corners (a pixel cut off every outer corner), slightly bouncy (some letters sit 1 px low) | tabular, 7 wide | 13 |
| **Pip** (small; `drawSmall`, `smallWidth`, `SMALL_H`) | prices on tags, keycap letters, chip counts | 5 | caps only (lowercase draws as caps) | 2 px verticals | tabular, 4 wide | 7 |

- Keep every `ICON` code point and its meaning (the code uses them everywhere), redrawn in Reed's
  weight. Keep the API names so existing calls keep working; `drawText` and friends simply become
  Reed. Add `drawBig`/`bigWidth`/`BIG_H`/`BIG_LINE_H` and `ellipsize` stays.
- Glyph coverage: the font test (`tests/engine.test.ts`) must still pass for all three.
- A contact sheet (`pixel-art/fonts/out/fonts.png`) shows the three fonts beside the S&S font and a
  crop of Last Watt's HUD, so the difference is checked by eye.

## Rich text (`src/ui/rich.ts`)

One markup for every string a player reads, parsed once and cached:

- `{i:ironOre}` an 8x8 item icon inline; `{u:research}` an 8x8 UI icon (`ui_icons_small` names)
- `{k:T}` a keycap (Pip letter on a little key); `{k:LMB}` / `{k:RMB}` / `{k:WHEEL}` mouse glyphs
- `{coin:25}` a coin chip (coin glyph + the number in coin ink); `{coin}` the glyph alone
- `{good:…}` `{bad:…}` `{warn:…}` `{coinc:…}` `{research:…}` `{power:…}` `{reset:…}` `{soft:…}`
  colour spans; `{big:…}` a Bullfrog span. No nesting.
- `→` `×` `+` `/min` are plain characters.
- `drawRich(ui, s, x, y, maxW, color?) → height`, `richWidth(s)`, `wrapRich(s, maxW)`; icons and
  keycaps never split from the word after them; the overlap audit records each run.
- Plain strings without braces draw exactly as text, so old strings still work while they are
  rewritten.

## The kit (`src/ui/ui.ts`, `src/app/chrome.ts`, `src/app/widgets.ts`)

New panel styles, each code-drawn first and swapped for the art director's 9-slices when present
(`hasArt`), so nothing waits on art:

- **tag**: a paper tag, deckled (1 px notches) on its free edges, a punched hole with a string to
  its anchor (a world point or a HUD object), a soft ink shadow down-right. Hover reports, the
  wheel's info, the floor's numbers, toasts.
- **bubble**: a cream speech bubble with an ink outline, rounded 2 px corners and a tail toward the
  speaker (any of four sides, any offset). Goals, hints, the cast's lines.
- **ribbon**: a moss (or the role's ink colour) ribbon with forked ends and a lit fold; titles,
  trophies, celebrations, tabs.
- **sign**: a wooden plank on two ropes (the Purse).
- **book**: the ledger, two paper pages over a leather cover with a spine shadow (full-screen
  menus).
- **keycap**, **chip** (a rounded pill: icon + number, role-coloured ink on a tint of paper),
  **bar** (an ink-outlined trough on paper with a notch for a target), **stamp** (red ink).
- `plate()` becomes paper; `frame()` becomes the book or a ribbon; `card()` becomes a tag with a
  ribbon title; `button()` becomes a paper button with an ink outline that presses down 1 px
  (`gold` buttons: brass with ink text). The default text colour on any of these is `T.ink`.
- `menuTemplate()` keeps its signature and its returned `{ body, detail, free }`, and draws the
  book: the menu's own tabs as bookmark ribbons along the top edge, the title in Bullfrog at the top
  of the left page, the detail panel as the right page. Every screen moves to it.
- The tooltip (`ui.tip`) is a tag. `TipLine` text may carry markup.

- **The Purse** (top-left): a wooden sign on two ropes; the coin counter in Bullfrog at 2x, gold
  digits with an ink outline (they roll, bump and sparkle as now). A paper tag hangs under it with
  the rate (`+7.0K/min`); hovering the Purse hangs the Shipping tag (what sold, per minute, icons).
  Nothing else lives on the Purse. Machine Kits show where they are spent (the wheel's module
  slots, the Modules page, the stamp). Patents show on the Patents page, the Reorganize page, and
  in the Committee's bubble when a Reorganize would pay. Power shows on the floor.
- **The trouble bell** (under the Purse): appears only while a machine has a problem; swings and
  rings when a new problem starts; a red-ink count tag. Click: the camera pans to the next problem
  and its tag opens (the old line-health chip's job). Problems off the screen get a red edge arrow
  pointing at them (as Sparks get a gold one).
- **The lift** (top-right): a brass dial in a wooden case, a needle pointing at W or F, each floor a
  brass button; a dot on a floor that wants you. Click a button, or `Tab`. Locked floors are
  padlocked buttons.
- **Slips** (under the lift): toasts as paper slips that slide in, rest, and slide out. Trophies drop
  as a ribbon from the top centre with the trophy icon. While a page is open, slips and ribbons
  wait (the last four and two are kept) and arrive when it closes, so nothing covers a page.
- **Bramble's corner** (bottom-left): the speaker's portrait (48x48, on a lily pad) and the speech
  bubble with the current goal, rich text, its tail at the portrait. Click the bubble to fold it to
  the portrait (a `!` bubble when a new line arrives); click the portrait to unfold. When the goal
  points at a tile, an arrow on the floor bobs over it (as now) and the bubble may hold a small
  `{u:…}` pointing where. Other speakers take the corner for their lines (below).
- **The notebook** (bottom-right): a leather notebook with ribbon bookmarks; click opens the book
  at the last page; a dot when a page wants you. Its pages are every full-screen menu: Research,
  Orders, Modules, Codex, Survey, Megaproject, Patents, Drawer, Stats, Trophies, Options, Keys. The
  book shows them as ribbon tabs down its right edge. Keys still open pages directly (`T`, `O`,
  `M`, …).
- **The newspaper** (bottom-right, beside the notebook): the Swamp Gazette folded, today's headline
  on a slip beside it in Reed (it wraps to two lines before it cuts, so the joke's last words
  survive); flips to the next headline every ~15 s with a paper sound; news about your
  factory gets a red "EXTRA!" corner. Click opens the Gazette page (the recent headlines). This
  replaces the crawl.
- **World doors**: the Dock opens Orders, the Hopper opens Research, the Bay opens the Megaproject,
  and the town's buildings open their pages where they exist (Unit 7's observatory: the Codex;
  Voltaire's pavilion: her orders). A hovered door shows its tag.

## The floor's numbers (tags on the floor)

Drawn in world space each frame, anchored to tiles, culled to the view, never covering their own
anchor tile; they shrink to an icon + number at zoom 1 and grow at 2x+; they don't stack on each
other (a simple nudge). Each is a small paper tag on a string from its anchor:

- **Dock**: `+5.1K/min` coin (with a coin chip), and the active order's portrait + per-item bars;
  its flag still flies.
- **Hopper**: the research node's icon, name and %, its ring as now.
- **Lines**: what arrives at the Dock and the Hopper, as an inflow row on their own tags (the top
  four items by rate, each icon with its `/min` from the delivery stats; a starved line's `0` in
  warn ink). Built first as a tag at each belt's mouth: at a busy Dock six tags stacked over the
  Dock's own, so they joined it.
- **Grids** (Act II): over each grid's first producer, `{u:power} 82%` with supply/demand on hover;
  brownout turns it warn ink.
- **The Bay** (Factory): the Megaproject's stage and %.
- **Audits**: the existing badge becomes the Inspector's clipboard tag on the box.

## The Workbench Wheel (`src/app/wheel.ts`, replacing `buildbar.ts`)

- **Open**: right-click (a tap, under 6 px of movement; a right drag still pans) on the floor, or
  `Q`/`Space`, opens it centred at the cursor (clamped on screen). A small toolbox button beside
  Bramble's corner opens it at the screen's centre, for mouse-only players and for the first goal.
- **Hub**: a wooden disc; the last building picked sits in it (click the hub: pick it again).
- **Petals** (inner ring, radius ~30 UI px): the categories that exist, each a round brass button
  with its icon: Tools (Rotate, Remove, Fold, Flow), Build, Power, Heavy, Upgrades; on the Factory
  floor Tools, Trunk, Modules. A new thing inside a petal gives it a dot.
- **Items** (outer arc, radius ~58): hovering or clicking a petal fans its items out as paper
  discs with the sprite at 1x and a Pip price tag hanging off each; affordable ones get a gold
  rim, new ones a dot, locked ones a padlock. The goal's item pulses.
- **Info tag**: the hovered item's tag beside the wheel: name in Bullfrog, its recipe as an icon
  equation and rate, its price chip, its one-line flavour, and what unlocks it if locked.
- Picking an item closes the wheel and the ghost follows the cursor as now. Right-tap or `Esc`
  with a building held drops it; right-tap again opens the wheel. Number keys `1`-`9` still pick
  within the last opened petal (hidden accelerators, never drawn as a numbered row). `R` rotates,
  `X` removes, `F` folds, `L` flow, as now.
- Upgrades (the Bellows, land) live in their petal; land chunks for sale also get a "for sale"
  sign tag on the floor with the price chip (click it to buy, as now).

## Reports: tags on strings

The hover report and the pinned report are the same tag (pinned: a pushpin, it stays):

```
 ╭ HAND FURNACE ─────────── {i:ironIngot} ╮
 │ {bad:CHOKED}  slag has no way out       │   status chip + its 3-6 word cause
 │ {i:ironOre} → {i:ironIngot}  27/min     │   the recipe, the rate now
 │ ▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓▓░  of 30/min                   │   bar against its rate when busy
 │ in  {i:ironOre} 30/min ← Miner          │   who feeds it, how much
 │ side {i:slag} 1 per 4 → {bad:nowhere}   │   the byproduct's route
 │ {k:LMB} shovel   {k:R} turn   {k:X} sell │   verbs
 ╰────────────────────────────────────────╯
```

Status chips: `WORKING` (good), `STARVED` (warn, names the missing item with its icon and how short),
`BLOCKED` / `FULL` / `CHOKED` (bad, names where the output can't go), `NO POWER` (power), `BROWNOUT
82%`, `OVERDRIVE 3x 6s`, `COOLING 12s`. The words come from the report's data, not from parsing
`src/sim/analysis.ts`'s prose; that file's sentences stay for the tests and the offline report's
history, and the app builds the rows from the same state (`src/app/tags.ts`).

## Full-screen menus: the ledger book

Every screen draws in `menuTemplate` (the book). Content: ink on paper, Bullfrog headings, icon
rows, chips and bars; lists ruled with faint `paperLo` lines; selected rows get a pencil underline,
not a highlight block. The research tree stays a tree but drawn as pencil on paper (nodes are
paper discs with the node icon, done ones stamped with a green tick, the selected one circled in
red pencil). The grade card is the Inspector's report slip with the grade stamped in red ink. The
Reorganize page is a contract the Committee slides across the table (you gain / you keep / you
lose as three columns of ink, the "sign here" button). The offline report is a letter from
Bramble.

## The voice guide

Speech bubbles only. Short (aim 6-12 words, never past ~14), present tense, one idea, at most one
keycap and one price. The game's numbers go in chips, not in the sentence.

- **Mayor Bramble** (frog, the town's mayor, your first customer; the default speaker): warm,
  eager, exclamatory, pleased by small things. Calls the furnace "she". "Ribbit" at most once every
  five lines. *"Click the rocks! Ore, please!"* · *"Smells like money. Get the Bellows! {coin:25}"* ·
  *"She's choking on slag! {k:LMB} shovel, or a {i:slag} Heap."* · *"A whole line in one tile?
  Show me! {k:F}"* · *"Gizmos! The town is losing its mind!"*
- **Unit 7** (the collector robot; Codex, its orders): ALL CAPS, clipped, counts everything, quietly
  delighted. *"NEW SPECIMEN: GLASS. COLLECTION 14%. PLEASING."*
- **Dame Voltaire** (the inventor; her orders, Mk2, modes): grand, exacting, a little vain.
  *"Precision, darling. Forty circuits, not one crooked."*
- **The Committee** (three raccoons in a coat; Patents, Reorganize, the Drill's later stages): three
  voices in one bubble, lowercase, conspiratorial. *"psst." "we know a guy." "he files patents."*
- **The Inspector** (the frog in the bowler; audits, grades, Sparks): officious, clipboard-dry,
  secretly impressed. *"Seventy seconds. Do carry on."* · *"An S. I shall tell no one. Fine, everyone."*

Tags, wheel, menus and floor labels use no voice: nouns, icons, numbers, verbs on keys.

## What is a genre convention (flagged in DECISIONS.md)

A radial build menu (pie menus: The Sims, many strategy and builder games), diegetic menus (the Dock
opens orders), a mascot's speech-bubble tutorial (Animal Crossing's Isabelle, Kairosoft's advisors),
a newspaper headline (re-derived from Cookie Clicker's news ticker already), an alert bell
(re-derived from Factorio's alerts, the old chip), tags on strings for tooltips. None copies a
layout, art or wording from a specific game.
