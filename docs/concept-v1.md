# Concept v1 — the first expansion (2026-10-20)

The expansion of `docs/original-pitch.md` that renamed the game and introduced folding. Kept for
history; `PLAN.md` and `DESIGN.md` are the source of truth and win any conflict.

## What changed from the pitch

- **Renamed** Pocket Foundry → **Foldworks**, after the signature mechanic.
- **Added folding**: fold a working line into a one-tile module that produces at the line's
  measured rate; stamp copies; build the next floor (Workshop → Factory → District → World) out
  of modules. Solves scale without lag, gives a zoom-out progression, and rewards clean lines.
- **Added the Hopper** (Builderment-style): research costs item deliveries, not points.
- **Added byproducts** as a routing layer that changes meaning over time (nuisance → input →
  efficiency bonus).
- **Clicking evolves**: hand-mining → Overdrive → Inspiration, plus Sparks (map events).
- **Customers became a cast** (Mayor Bramble, Unit 7, Dame Voltaire, The Committee) with
  reputation tracks.
- **Megaprojects persist across resets**, the spine of the long game.
- **Three reset layers**: Reorganize (Patents), Relocate (Legacy, biome worlds), the Last Fold
  (Kernels).
- **Automation of automation** as a pillar: each layer automates the one below.
- **Cut or deferred**: community blueprint server and scoreboards (share strings instead),
  limited-time challenges (conflict with "respect the player's time"), efficiency ratings as a
  separate system (merged into fold audit grades).

## First slice

Act I only: an 8×8 grid growing to 24×24, five machines (miner, smelter, press, assembler,
belt), the Hopper with ~10 research nodes, one customer (Mayor Bramble), Sparks, and the first
fold. If folding feels good, everything else follows.
